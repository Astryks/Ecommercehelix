# SOP 14: List growth and campaign calendar

> **In plain words.** Grow your own email and SMS list so you rely less on paid ads. Then send to it at a steady pace: often enough to earn, not so often that people leave.

**Purpose.** Grow an owned audience (email and SMS) that lowers dependence on paid ads, and send campaigns at a steady rhythm that earns without burning the list.

**Copilot triggers**
- Pop-up sign-up rate below 3%.
- No campaign sent in 14+ days.
- 6 to 10 weeks before a big sale (list-building window).
- Friday retention day.

## Pop-up and forms

- Target a sign-up rate above 3%, and 6 to 10% with a strong offer.
- Two to three fields at most. Multi-step forms (email first, then phone, then a question) often add 2 to 3 points.
- Ask one zero-party question ("What are you shopping for?") to personalise the welcome flow.
- Offer options: dollar-off for high AOV, percent-off for low AOV, a high-value free gift to protect margin, early access, or a quiz result.
- Separate mobile and desktop designs. Make it easy to close on mobile.

## Other list builders

- **Competitions and giveaways:** run them when ad costs are lower (before peak season) to collect future buyers cheaply. Collaborations with complementary brands widen reach. Watch email quality: low-intent sweepstakes entrants buy less.
- **Lead ads** on Meta for list growth when CPMs are low.
- **Hype pages** before launches and sales (SOP 15).
- **Quizzes** for products with choice complexity.

An email address keeps paying a small amount every month for as long as the subscriber stays engaged, so cost per subscriber can be judged against the expected value per subscriber per month.

## Campaign rhythm

| Stage | Campaigns per week |
| --- | --- |
| Just starting | 2 a month, building to 1 a week |
| Stage 1 to 2 | 1 to 2 a week |
| Stage 2 to 3 | 2 to 4 a week, with segmentation |

Mix: product stories, education (Teach pillar), proof, founder notes, new arrivals, offers (keep offers a minority).

## Segments

- Engaged (opened or clicked in 30/60/90 days), purchasers, VIPs (top 10% by spend), window shoppers (viewed products recently, never bought), lapsed buyers.
- Send most campaigns to engaged segments. Mail the full list rarely.

## Win-back and clean-up

- Win-back flow for customers past their expected repurchase window.
- Sunset flow for profiles that never engage, then suppress them. A smaller engaged list delivers better.
