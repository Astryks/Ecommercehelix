# SOP 11: Landing pages and site CRO

> **In plain words.** Make more money from every visitor, which makes every ad cheaper. Fix things in this order. Make the site clear, then convincing, then easy to buy from. Then add ways to buy more.

**Purpose.** Raise revenue per visit so every channel becomes cheaper. Work in order: make the site clear, then convincing, then remove friction, then sell harder.

**Copilot triggers**
- RPV below benchmark (SOP 03 says "site problem").
- Audit fix list has open high-impact items.
- Thursday in the weekly rhythm (site day).
- An ad is engaging but converting poorly.

## The order of work

1. **Clear.** Can a new visitor understand what you sell and why it is different in 5 seconds?
2. **Convincing.** Proof, reasons why, objection answers.
3. **Frictionless.** Speed, policies, payment options, broken elements. Watch session recordings and heatmaps for rage clicks and dead ends.
4. **Selling.** Landing pages, A/B tests, better assets, offers.

Avoid high-effort, low-impact work. A cheap theme tweak shipped this week usually beats an expensive custom redesign.

## Page checklists

**Homepage (above the fold)**
- [ ] Promo bar with the current offer or shipping promise.
- [ ] Simple navigation, best sellers easy to reach. Visual menus on mobile.
- [ ] Top banner with one message and one call to action. Separate 4:5 mobile image.
- [ ] Email capture pop-up (timed, easy to close on mobile). As a rule of thumb, a pop-up that signs up about 3% of the people who see it is doing fine; good ones do better. Footer forms usually capture far fewer.

**Product page (treat it as a landing page)**
- [ ] One line under the title saying why this product is different.
- [ ] Offer block above the fold (bundle, gift, free shipping threshold).
- [ ] 3 to 5 reasons why, with icons or short lines.
- [ ] Curated reviews with photos near the top, all genuine reviews lower. Never hide or edit negative ones (SOP 16).
- [ ] FAQ answering the top objections (sizing, materials, shipping time, returns, how to use).
- [ ] Sticky add-to-cart on mobile.

**Collection pages**
- [ ] Multi-select filters with an easy reset.
- [ ] Best sellers first by default.
- Most small stores do not need paid search or merchandising tools.

**Cart and checkout**
- [ ] Progress bar to free shipping or gift.
- [ ] Express payment buttons.
- [ ] No surprise costs at the last step. Surprise extra costs (shipping, tax, fees) are one of the most common reasons shoppers abandon checkout.

## Landing pages

- The ad sells the click; the page sells the product.
- One message per page, matching the ad that sends traffic.
- Answer the key questions in order. What is it? Why should I care? Why trust you? Why now? What if it doesn't work?
- Formats that work: "N reasons why" listicle, comparison page, founder story, problem and solution, bundle builder.
- Build fast (48 hours with a page builder), test against the product page.

## Testing rules

- One change per test, 50/50 split, aim for about 95% confidence.
- Keep a test log: hypothesis, change, dates, result, decision.
- Monitor funnel steps: add-to-cart rate, reached-checkout rate, conversion rate.
- Small wins (2 to 5%) compound across all traffic.

## Speed and apps

- Only chase speed fixes that save 1 to 2+ seconds. Compress large images and videos.
- Every app adds script weight and monthly fees. Test cheap apps first; build custom only after an idea is proven.
