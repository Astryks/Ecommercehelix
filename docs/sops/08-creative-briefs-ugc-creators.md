# SOP 08: Creative briefs, UGC and creators

**Purpose.** Produce enough native-feeling creative every week without a big budget, using a simple brief format, a creator pipeline and the customer's own words.

**Copilot triggers**
- A creative test batch is due (SOP 07).
- The user has fewer than 10 unused ad-ready assets.
- The user asks "how do I find creators?" or "what should I film?".

## The one-page brief

1. **Goal:** which layer (Cold, Mixed, Warm) and which variable is being tested.
2. **Pillar:** Teach, Prove, Feel or Stand for.
3. **Customer and problem:** one sentence in the customer's language (pull from reviews and support tickets).
4. **Hook options:** three opening lines or visuals for the first 3 seconds.
5. **Key message:** one idea only.
6. **Proof:** review quote, demo, stat, before and after.
7. **Offer and call to action.**
8. **Format:** 9:16 video 15 to 45 seconds, 4:5 static, carousel, or creator talking head. Keep text inside platform safe zones.
9. **Must-show and must-avoid:** product shots, claims to avoid (health, before/after rules), brand dos and don'ts.

## Filming basics for founders and creators

- Steady camera, slight movement every few seconds.
- Audio matters more than video quality. Use a cheap lapel mic.
- Face the light, never a bright window behind you.
- Look at the lens, eyes on the top third line of the frame, fill at least a third of the frame.
- Add captions (burned in or uploaded) for sound-off viewing.

## Creator pipeline

1. **List:** 50 to 100 creators who look like your customer. Use platform creator marketplaces, tagged posts and existing customers.
2. **Gift at scale:** small brands can send dozens of gifts a month; roughly half usually post if the product is good. Track who posts.
3. **Pay the best:** offer a flat fee for ad-usage rights to the best performers.
4. **Affiliate:** give the creator a code (for example 10% off for their audience) and a commission (for example 15%).
5. **Partnership ads:** run creator posts as ads from the creator's handle. Platforms report lower acquisition costs when these sit inside normal campaigns. See the Trends feed for current data.
6. **Reuse:** use creator Q&A and comments to find objections and answer them in new ads.

## Briefs from customer words

- Mine one-star and three-star reviews of competitors for unmet needs. Brief ads that answer them.
- Same product, different problem: one product can solve several problems for different people. Each problem is a new ad.

## AI-assisted creative (rules)

- AI can draft scripts, hooks, copy variants, static layouts and resized versions.
- Any AI-generated person, voice or testimonial must be labelled where platforms require it, and must never fake a customer review.
- Review platform auto-enhancements (music, text overlays, generated backgrounds) and turn off any that hurt the brand.
