# SOP 16: Customer research and reviews

> **In plain words.** Ask customers why they bought and what nearly stopped them. Use their words in your ads, pages and offers, and collect reviews, because proof makes every future sale cheaper.

**Purpose.** Use the customer's own words to write offers, pages and ads that convert, and build the proof that lowers the cost of every future sale.

**Copilot triggers**
- Onboarding (first-week task).
- Before writing briefs, offers or landing pages.
- Saturday customer day.
- Fewer than 20 reviews on the best-selling product.

## Sources (cheapest first)

1. Product reviews (yours and competitors', especially 1 to 3 stars).
2. Support tickets, chat logs and DMs.
3. Ad comments.
4. Post-purchase survey: "What nearly stopped you buying?", "Where did you hear about us?", "What did you compare us with?".
5. 15-minute customer calls (5 a quarter is enough to learn a lot).
6. Community forums and social threads in the niche.

## Steps

1. Collect raw text into the research log (Helix can pull reviews and tickets when connected).
2. Tag each item: desire, fear, objection, use case, comparison, language worth reusing.
3. Count tags monthly. The top objections feed the offer (SOP 04) and the product page FAQ (SOP 11).
4. Pull 10 phrases customers use. Use them in hooks and headlines.
5. Write one "customer story" per month for email and ads.

## Reviews engine

- Ask at the right time (after delivery plus a few days of use), by email and SMS.
- Ask for photos and video; offer a small thank-you where platform rules allow.
- Reply to reviews, especially negative ones, quickly and kindly.
- Feature the best reviews on product pages, in ads (Prove pillar) and in emails.
- Ask happy customers for a Google review too, but follow platform rules (no incentivised Google reviews, no mass asking that triggers filters).

## Competitor watch (15 minutes a week)

- Check competitors' active ads in public ad libraries: new angles, offers, formats.
- Check their sale timing and offers.
- Log ideas worth adapting (never copy).

| Helix can do (with approval where it writes) | The user does |
| --- | --- |
