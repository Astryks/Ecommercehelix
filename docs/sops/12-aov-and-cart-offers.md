# SOP 12: Order value and cart offers

> **In plain words.** Get people to spend a bit more per order without giving away profit. Use bundles, a free shipping threshold a little above your usual order, and simple add-ons in the cart.

**Purpose.** Lift average order value without cutting margin, so each paid visit is worth more.

**Copilot triggers**
- RPV low and AOV below the category norm or below 1.5x the main product price.
- Free-shipping threshold not set, or set below current AOV.
- Planning a sale or launch.

## Tools, in the order to try

1. **Tiered cart offers.** For example: free shipping just above current AOV, a gift higher up, and a discount at the top. For high AOV stores, use dollar-off tiers.
2. **Gift with purchase.** High perceived value, low landed cost. Show it on every page (a small fly-in or banner), not just at checkout.
3. **Bundles.** Starter kits, "complete the look", multi-packs. Name them and give them their own product page.
4. **Post-purchase upsell.** One-click offer after payment for a complementary item. The first order is already placed, so it cannot hurt conversion.
5. **Cross-sell on product page and cart.** Two or three relevant add-ons only.
6. **Quantity breaks** for consumables.

## Steps

1. Pull AOV distribution: what share of orders are single-item? What is the median?
2. Set the first tier about 20 to 30% above current AOV (1.2 to 1.3 times AOV, as in Lesson 14.10). Check it sits above the median order too.
3. Check margin at each tier with the unit economics (SOP 02).
4. Launch one change at a time and watch AOV, conversion rate and RPV for 2 weeks.
5. Review tiers quarterly or when prices change.

## Decision rules

- Judge on RPV, not AOV alone. A tier that lifts AOV but drops conversion more is a loss.
- Gift-with-purchase logic must be tested (threshold edge cases, multiple gifts, discount stacking).
- Buy-one-get-one offers should discount the cheaper item.

## Who does what

| Helix can do (with approval) | The user does |
| --- | --- |
| Analyse order value distribution and recommend tiers | Choose tiers and gifts |
| Draft cart offer settings and banner copy | Configure or approve the app or Shopify discount |
| Watch RPV impact and report | Decide to keep or revert |
