# SOP 10: TikTok and emerging channels

**Purpose.** Decide when a new channel is worth it and launch it without wasting money. Covers TikTok ads, TikTok Shop, Pinterest, AI shopping surfaces and AI search visibility.

**Copilot triggers**
- The main channel is profitable and the user wants to grow beyond it.
- The user asks "should I be on TikTok?", "what about ChatGPT ads?", or "how do I show up in AI search?".
- Quarterly channel review.

## Rule zero

Add a channel only when (1) current channels are profitable, (2) there is creative to feed it, and (3) the owner can check it at least every other day. New channels often start at around 10% of total ad budget and are judged on their effect on total MER, not just in-platform numbers.

## TikTok ads (website sales)

1. **Organic first.** Post native content for a few weeks to learn which styles get traction. Build a bank of 10 to 15 ad-ready videos before spending.
2. **Two campaigns.** Run one campaign optimising for an earlier action (view content or add to cart) alongside the purchase campaign, unless spend is high. Turning off the earlier-action campaign often raises purchase cost because the platform gets fewer signals.
3. **Budget type.** Campaign-level budget lets the platform pick ad sets. Ad-set budget gives you control when testing creative styles.
4. **Fatigue is fast.** Check every day or two. A creative can go from fine to very expensive within 3 to 4 days. Use leading metrics (cost per add to cart, cost per click, 25% and 50% view rates) to spot it early.
5. **When to turn off.** Turn off ads that take a large share of budget at a poor cost per purchase so the budget moves to better ads. Judge the whole channel against total MER.
6. **Next steps when it works.** More iterations of winning hooks, catalog or shopping campaigns, bundles for AOV.

## TikTok Shop

In 2026 TikTok Shop ads run through automated GMV-based campaigns that pull in paid, organic and affiliate content. Treat in-platform return as a channel score, not comparable to Meta or Google. Affiliates and creator content are the fuel. Decide first whether selling in-app fits your margin after commissions, fees and coupons.

## Pinterest

Strong for planned purchases (home, wedding, fashion, gifting). Search-like behaviour, long content life. Start with a catalog and 10 to 20 fresh pins a week; judge over longer windows.

## AI shopping surfaces and AI search

- Shopping assistants and AI search now recommend products. Shopify stores may be discoverable by default through agentic storefront programs. Paid placements in chat assistants are opening up via product-feed ads.
- **Visibility basics:** clear product data (titles, attributes, prices, availability, return policy), plain-language FAQs on product pages, consistent brand facts across your site and other sites, reviews, and allowing reputable AI crawlers.
- **Classic SEO still feeds this:** commercial and transactional keyword research first, one main heading per page, meta descriptions around 150 to 160 characters, logical site structure, fast mobile pages.
- Track AI-referred orders separately in analytics.
