# Module 9: Website, conversion and order value

**Outcome:** a store that turns more visits into revenue: clear, convincing, frictionless and good at lifting order value.

**Related SOPs:** [01 Audit](../sops/01-store-audit.md), [11 CRO](../sops/11-landing-pages-and-cro.md), [12 AOV](../sops/12-aov-and-cart-offers.md)


<!-- plain:start -->
> **In plain words**
>
> **What it is:** How to turn more of your visitors into buyers, and get bigger orders.
>
> **Why it matters:** If more visitors buy, every ad dollar goes further without spending more.
>
> **Do this:**
>
> 1. Make the top of each product page clear: what it is, price, reviews, add to cart.
> 2. Remove anything that slows checkout and turn on express payment buttons.
> 3. Set a free shipping amount a bit above your average order.
>
> **Words to know:**
>
> - **Conversion rate:** Out of every 100 visits, how many end in an order. 2% means 2 orders per 100 visits.
> - **Above the fold:** The part of a page you see before you scroll.
> - **Free shipping threshold:** The order amount where shipping becomes free, like 'Free shipping over $100'.
> - **AOV:** Average order value: total sales divided by number of orders.
>
> All words are explained in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Lesson 9.1: The improvement system
1. **Gap analysis:** score each page against the checklists below. List gaps.
2. **Development plan:** rank gaps by impact ÷ effort. Group into quick wins, small builds, big builds.
3. **Build:** apps or theme settings first, custom code only after an idea is proven.
4. **Measure:** heatmaps, recordings, funnel reports, A/B tests.
5. Repeat monthly.

## Lesson 9.2: Page checklists
**Homepage above the fold:** promo bar, simple nav, a top banner with one message (separate mobile image), clear call to action, trust cue.
**Homepage below the fold:** best sellers, categories, reasons to buy, reviews, founder or story, press, email sign-up.
**Product page above the fold:** title, one-line difference, star rating, price with anchor or bundle value, offer block, variant selector, add to cart, delivery estimate, key trust badges.
**Product page below the fold:** reasons why, how to use, photo reviews, FAQs, comparison, guarantees, cross-sells.
**Collections:** filters with multi-select and easy reset, sort by best selling, badges (new, best seller, low stock), quick add.
**Cart and checkout:** progress to free shipping or gift, upsell slot, express payments, no surprise fees, trust badges, policy links. Remove optional add-ons that frustrate (for example pre-ticked shipping protection) if they hurt conversion.

## Lesson 9.3: Accessibility basics
Colour contrast, alt text on images, keyboard navigation, readable font sizes, labelled form fields, captions on video. Good for customers and often for search.

## Lesson 9.4: Speed
Compress images and videos, remove unused apps and scripts, lazy-load below-the-fold media. Only chase fixes saving 1 to 2+ seconds. Manage bot traffic that skews analytics and strains the site.

## Lesson 9.5: Copy that sells
Lead with the customer's outcome, be specific, use their words, answer objections, keep sentences short, use subheads and bullets, end with a clear call to action.

## Lesson 9.6: Policies and service
Sales terms page (what is excluded, dates), plain-language returns, shipping times by region, visible contact options, help centre with top questions, automations for order status and FAQs. Customer service is a conversion tool.

## Lesson 9.7: Landing pages
Wireframe: hook headline -> problem -> solution -> 3 to 5 reasons -> proof -> offer -> FAQ -> final call to action. Types: listicle, comparison, founder story, bundle builder, promotional collection page (also useful for search). Build in 48 hours with a page builder and test against the product page.

## Lesson 9.8: Measurement
- Heatmaps and session recordings: rage clicks, dead clicks, scroll depth.
- Store reports: sessions, conversion funnel (sessions -> add to cart -> reached checkout -> purchase), top landing pages, device split.
- Analytics: traffic sources, landing page conversion, AI referrals.
- When conversion drops suddenly: check tracking, site errors, payment methods, stock, pricing changes, traffic mix, competitor sales.

## Lesson 9.9: A/B testing
One change, 50/50 split, about 95% confidence, keep a log. Test ideas: menu layouts, cart drawer vs page, gift-with-purchase display, video on product page, returns messaging, pre-sale page variants. Small wins compound.

## Lesson 9.10: Order value
Tiers (free shipping -> gift -> discount), bundles and bundle builders, cross-sells in cart, post-purchase one-click upsell, quantity breaks, subscriptions for consumables (subscribe and save, flexible skip). Judge by RPV.

## Lesson 9.11: Shipping psychology
Free shipping thresholds lift AOV; set just above median order value. Show delivery estimates. Offer express as a paid upgrade. Customers hate surprise shipping costs at checkout.

## Lesson 9.12: Promotions without destroying margin
Prefer gifts, bundles, VIP early access and loyalty rewards over blanket discounts. If you want to step back from constant discounting, do it gradually: fewer sales, stronger value messaging, better offers to new customers only.

## Lesson 9.13: Special cases
- **High-ticket and long consideration:** more content, comparisons, finance options, samples or swatches, consultations, longer retargeting windows.
- **Preorders and made to order:** clear dispatch dates, deposit options, progress updates.
- **Video commerce:** shoppable videos on product pages and homepage.
- **Reviews:** a wall of customer love, photo reviews, review requests timed after use.
- **Search and discovery:** synonyms, best-seller boosting, no dead-end searches.
- **Metafields and structured content:** consistent product details, better filters and AI readability.
- **B2B:** wholesale pricing behind login, separate catalogs.
- **International:** local currency and duties handling (Module 15).

## Lesson 9.14: Working with developers
App evaluation (cost, speed impact, support, lock-in), customisation cost estimates, choose a developer by portfolio and a small paid test, write a development brief (goal, user story, design, acceptance criteria, deadline), and consider a re-theme only when the current theme blocks multiple priority fixes.

## Lesson 9.15: Category patterns from store audits
| Category | Usual gaps |
| --- | --- |
| Apparel | Size guidance, fit reviews, model info, outfit cross-sells |
| Kids and baby | Safety proof, age guidance, gifting bundles |
| Beauty and supplements | Ingredient clarity, results timeline, before and after rules, subscriptions |
| Accessories and jewellery | Scale photos, gift packaging, personalisation |
| Home and living | Dimensions, room context, swatches, delivery clarity |
| Food | Taste descriptions, freshness, bundles, subscriptions |
| Pets | Pet-specific proof, refills |
