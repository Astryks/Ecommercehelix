# Module 12: TikTok, Pinterest, SEO and AI search

**Outcome:** you know when and how to add channels beyond Meta and Google, and how to be found in search and AI assistants.

**Related SOP:** [10](../sops/10-tiktok-and-emerging-channels.md)


<!-- plain:start -->
> **In plain words**
>
> **What it is:** When to add TikTok, Pinterest and search, and how to be found by AI assistants.
>
> **Why it matters:** New channels can help, but only after Meta and Google are working. Add one at a time.
>
> **Do this:**
>
> 1. Only start a new channel once your main ads are profitable.
> 2. Reuse your best ads, edited for the new channel.
> 3. Write clear product titles and descriptions so search engines understand them.
>
> **Words to know:**
>
> - **SEO:** Changes that help your store show up in Google without paying for ads.
> - **Meta description:** The short summary under your page title in Google results.
> - **Organic:** Unpaid: posts, search results and word of mouth that you do not pay to show.
>
> All words are explained in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Lesson 12.1: TikTok readiness
Post organically first, learn what works, build a bank of 10 to 15 ad-ready videos. Set up a business centre, ad account, pixel and events, and columns (spend, CPA, add to cart, cost per add to cart, CTR, CPC, 2-second and 6-second views, 25/50/75/100% views).

## Lesson 12.2: Structures by spend
| Daily spend | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| About $100 | One purchase campaign plus a small earlier-event campaign (view content or add to cart) |
| About $200 | Add a catalog or shopping campaign |
| About $300+ | Add landing-page traffic or video-view campaigns for cheap reach, separate tests by creative style |

Campaign budget lets the platform allocate; ad group budget lets you control tests.

## Lesson 12.3: Spark ads and creators
Spark ads run organic posts (yours or creators') as ads with their engagement. Get authorisation codes from creators. Creator content is the main fuel.

## Lesson 12.4: Optimising TikTok
- Check every day or two: fatigue can arrive within 3 to 4 days.
- Watch leading metrics (cost per add to cart, CPC, view rates) to spot burnout early.
- Turn off ads that hog budget at poor CPA.
- If CPA rises after removing an earlier-event campaign, bring it back for signal volume.
- When things work: iterate hooks, try catalog or video shopping ads, bundles for AOV, then horizontal scaling.
- Judge the channel by total MER; new channels often start at about 10% of budget.

## Lesson 12.5: TikTok search
People search TikTok like a search engine. Use keywords in captions, on-screen text and voice-over. Answer common questions in videos.

## Lesson 12.6: TikTok Shop
GMV-based automated campaigns, affiliates, live shopping. Check margins after commissions and fees before committing.

## Lesson 12.7: Pinterest
- Organic: fresh pins weekly, keyword-rich titles and descriptions, boards by theme and season, long content life.
- Ads: catalog shopping, consideration then conversion campaigns, seasonal planning early (people plan ahead).
- Measurement: longer consideration windows; view and engagement conversions matter; compare with total MER.

## Lesson 12.8: SEO step by step
1. **Research:** commercial and transactional keywords first; volume, difficulty, intent; 10 keywords per category seed.
2. **On-site:** one H1 per page, logical H2/H3, meta titles and descriptions (about 150 to 160 characters), keyword-led collection pages, internal links, image alt text.
3. **Content:** buying guides, comparisons, how-tos mapped to the keywords.
4. **Technical:** speed, mobile, structured data, sitemap, no broken links or duplicate pages, search console checks.
5. **Off-site:** brand mentions, press, partnerships.

## Lesson 12.9: AI search and agentic commerce
- Answer-engine optimisation: be the clearest, most trusted answer (expertise, reviews, consistent brand facts).
- Allow reputable AI crawlers; clean product data; FAQ blocks; policy pages.
- Stores may be discoverable in AI shopping assistants; product-feed ads in chat assistants are emerging.
- Brand presence in peer platforms (forums, review sites, video) feeds AI answers.
- Track AI referrals in analytics.

## Lesson 12.10: Instagram shopping
Product tagging, shop setup, catalog sync, tagged Reels and posts.
