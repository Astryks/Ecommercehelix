# Module 12: TikTok, Pinterest, SEO and AI search

**Outcome:** you know when and how to add channels beyond Meta and Google, and how to be found in Google search and in AI assistants.

**Related SOP:** [10 TikTok and emerging channels](../sops/10-tiktok-and-emerging-channels.md)

## Lesson 12.1: TikTok readiness
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** TikTok can bring cheap attention, but only once you have videos that work there and tracking in place. Getting ready first stops you paying to learn what free posts could teach you. Only start TikTok ads once Meta and Google are profitable.

**Step 1.** Post free (organic) TikTok videos 3 to 5 times a week for a month to learn what works for you.

**Expected result:** You know which video styles get views and comments.

**Step 2.** Save the 10 to 15 best videos that are good enough to run as ads.

**Expected result:** You have a bank of ad-ready videos.

**Step 3.** Create a TikTok Business Center, an ad account, and install the TikTok pixel (it reports views, carts and sales) through the TikTok app in Shopify.

**Expected result:** TikTok Events Manager shows Purchase events coming in.

**Step 4.** In TikTok Ads Manager, set custom columns: spend, CPA, add to cart, cost per add to cart, CTR, CPC (cost per click), 2-second and 6-second views, and 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% watched.

**Expected result:** One saved view shows every number you need.

## Lesson 12.2: Structures by spend
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** TikTok structures depend on spend, just like Meta. Too many campaigns on a small budget starves each one.

**Step 1.** Find your daily spend band in the table below and set up the campaigns listed for it.

**Expected result:** Your TikTok account matches your budget.

**Step 2.** Choose campaign budget (TikTok shares money between ad groups) for steady running, or ad group budget when you need control over each test.

**Expected result:** Each campaign uses the budget type that suits its job.

### Good to know

| Daily spend | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| About $100 | One purchase campaign, plus a small campaign aimed at an earlier step (view content or add to cart). The earlier step gives TikTok more data to learn from. |
| About $200 | Add a catalog or shopping campaign. |
| About $300 or more | Add a traffic campaign to a landing page, or a video-view campaign, for cheap reach. Test each creative style separately. |

## Lesson 12.3: Spark ads and creators
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Spark ads run normal TikTok posts, yours or a creator's, as ads, keeping the post's likes and comments. Creator videos are the main fuel for TikTok ads (Module 19).

**Step 1.** Ask the creator to generate an authorisation code in their TikTok settings (**Creator tools > Ad settings**) and send it to you.

**Expected result:** You have a code for each post you want to run.

**Step 2.** In TikTok Ads Manager, create an ad, choose **Spark ad** and paste the code.

**Expected result:** The creator's post runs as an ad with its likes and comments.

## Lesson 12.4: Optimising TikTok
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** TikTok ads tire fast, sometimes in 3 to 4 days, so they need closer watching than Meta. Early signs warn you before cost per sale gets worse.

**Step 1.** Check TikTok every day or two and watch early warning signs: rising CPC, rising cost for each add to cart, and falling video watch rates.

**Expected result:** You spot tired ads before CPA rises.

**Step 2.** Turn off ads that eat budget with a poor cost per sale.

**Expected result:** Spend moves to working ads.

**Step 3.** If cost per sale rises after you turn off an earlier-step campaign (for example add to cart), turn it back on.

**Expected result:** TikTok gets the data it needs again.

**Step 4.** When things work, make new hooks, try catalog or video shopping ads, use bundles to raise order size, then add new campaigns.

**Expected result:** Growth comes from new creative and campaigns, not just more budget.

**Step 5.** Judge TikTok by your total MER on the Dashboard, starting at about 10% of your total ad budget.

**Expected result:** You see TikTok's effect on the whole store.

## Lesson 12.5: TikTok search
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Many people use TikTok like a search engine. Saying and showing your keywords helps your videos get found for months.

**Step 1.** Say your main keyword in the first line of the voice-over.

**Expected result:** TikTok can hear what the video is about.

**Step 2.** Put the keyword in on-screen text and the caption.

**Expected result:** The keyword appears in sound, text and caption.

**Step 3.** Make videos answering common questions about your product type.

**Expected result:** You show up when people search those questions.

## Lesson 12.6: TikTok Shop
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** TikTok Shop lets people buy without leaving TikTok, with automated campaigns that aim for total sales (GMV), affiliate creators and live shopping. Commission and fees can eat margin, so check the maths first.

**Step 1.** Work out your margin per order after TikTok Shop's commission and fees.

**Expected result:** You know your profit per order on TikTok Shop.

**Step 2.** If margin holds up, list your top 5 products and invite affiliate creators.

**Expected result:** Creators can sell your products from their videos.

## Lesson 12.7: Pinterest
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Pinterest pins keep getting found for months, much longer than social posts, and people plan purchases there well in advance. It rewards steady posting and early seasonal planning.

**Step 1.** Add fresh pins every week with search words in titles and descriptions, on boards by theme and season.

**Expected result:** Your pins show up in Pinterest search.

**Step 2.** Connect your catalog and run catalog shopping ads, starting with a consideration campaign, then a conversion campaign.

**Expected result:** Pinterest ads show your products with prices.

**Step 3.** Plan seasonal ads 2 to 3 months early.

**Expected result:** Your seasonal pins are live while people plan.

<!-- do:calendar -->

**Step 4.** Measure with a longer attribution window, counting sales after views and saves as well as clicks, and compare with your total MER.

**Expected result:** You judge Pinterest fairly for its slower decisions.

## Lesson 12.8: SEO step by step
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** SEO (search engine optimisation) means getting found in Google without paying for ads. It takes months to build but keeps paying. Five steps cover most of it.

**Step 1.** Research keywords: start with buying searches ("buy linen shirt", "best linen shirt"), check volume, difficulty (how hard to rank) and intent, and pick about 10 per category.

**Expected result:** Each category has a list of target keywords.

**Step 2.** Fix your pages: one main heading (H1) per page, clear subheadings (H2, H3), a page title and a meta description (the summary under your link in Google, about 150 to 160 characters) for each page, collection pages built around a keyword, links between your own pages and alt text on images.

**Expected result:** Every key page has a keyword-led title, meta description and one H1.

**Step 3.** Write helpful content that matches your keywords: buying guides, comparisons and how-tos.

**Expected result:** You publish at least one helpful article a month.

**Step 4.** Check the technical side in Google Search Console: fast mobile pages, structured data (code telling Google price, rating and stock), a submitted sitemap, no broken links and no duplicate pages.

**Expected result:** Search Console shows no errors and your sitemap is indexed.

**Step 5.** Get mentioned on other sites through press, partnerships and brand mentions.

**Expected result:** Other sites link to you.

## Lesson 12.9: AI search and agentic commerce
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** More shoppers now ask AI assistants (like ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity) what to buy, and some assistants can shop for them (agentic commerce). Clear facts, reviews and clean product data decide whether they recommend you.

**Step 1.** Make your brand facts the same everywhere (site, marketplaces, social bios) and grow your reviews.

**Expected result:** AI assistants find consistent facts and plenty of proof.

**Step 2.** Check your robots.txt lets trusted AI crawlers read your site, keep product data clean, and add FAQ sections and clear shipping and returns pages.

**Expected result:** AI tools can read and quote your store accurately.

**Step 3.** Keep your product feed connected so your store can show up inside AI shopping assistants.

**Expected result:** Your products are eligible for AI shopping results.

**Step 4.** Get talked about on forums, review sites and videos, and track AI assistant visits in Google Analytics (referral sources).

**Expected result:** You know how much traffic AI assistants send.

## Lesson 12.10: Instagram shopping
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Instagram shopping lets people tap a product in a post and buy. It is free once your catalog is connected.

**Step 1.** In Meta Commerce Manager, set up your shop and connect your product catalog.

**Expected result:** Commerce Manager shows your shop as approved.

**Step 2.** Turn on catalog sync from your store so prices and stock stay current.

**Expected result:** Catalog changes appear within a day.

**Step 3.** Tag products in your posts and Reels.

**Expected result:** Shoppers can tap to see and buy products.
