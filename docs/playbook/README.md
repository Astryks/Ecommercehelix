# The Helix Playbook

A complete, original course for online store owners. Each module has lessons with steps, checklists, examples and a self-check. Helix links daily tasks to the lesson that explains them, so you learn while you work.

**Plain words first.** Every module opens with a short "In plain words" box: what it is, why it matters, three steps to do, and the words to know. Any tricky word is explained in one line in the [glossary](../glossary.md). Modules 23 to 28 form the "Run your business" set, also shown as a hub in the app at /dashboard/business with dated admin reminders. The setup lessons (Meta in modules 7 and 20, Google in 8 and 20, Shopify in 1, 9 and 22) include numbered drawings that show where to click, with links to the official help pages. The drawings live in `public/guides/` and are made by `scripts/build_guides.py`; the plain-words boxes and drawing embeds are added by `scripts/plain_layer.py`.
