# Module 10: Email, SMS and messaging

**Outcome:** flows that earn every day, a campaign rhythm that does not burn the list, and messaging channels used where they add.

**Related SOPs:** [13](../sops/13-email-sms-flows.md), [14](../sops/14-list-growth-and-campaigns.md)

---

## Lesson 10.1: Sign-up forms
- Incentive options: dollar off (high AOV), percent off (low AOV), free gift, early access, competition entry.
- Micro-commitment forms: first a one-click question ("What are you shopping for?"), then email, then phone.
- Gamified forms (spin to win) can lift sign-ups; watch discount cost and list quality.
- Location-based forms show region-specific offers or SMS opt-ins.
- Targeting: delay a few seconds or on scroll; exit intent on desktop; suppress for subscribers.
- Test one thing at a time; aim above 3%, 6 to 10% with a strong offer.

## Lesson 10.2: The five core flows and extras
Welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, checkout abandonment, post-purchase (timings in SOP 13). Then: win-back, back in stock, price drop, replenishment, VIP, birthday, sunset, review request, cross-sell after first purchase.

## Lesson 10.3: Writing emails that get clicks
- Plain-text founder emails mixed with designed ones.
- Images inside plain-text style emails can work.
- Countdown timers for real deadlines (show a message when expired, not zeros).
- Zero-party data from forms to personalise (product interest, skin type, size).
- One main call to action per email.

## Lesson 10.4: Segments
Engaged 30/60/90 days, purchasers, repeat buyers, VIPs, window shoppers (recent viewers, no purchase), lapsed, by product interest. Most campaigns to engaged segments.

## Lesson 10.5: Campaign cadence and ideas
Build from 2 a month to weekly to 2 to 4 a week. Ideas: new arrivals, education, customer stories, behind the scenes, founder notes, gift guides, how-to, myth busting, user content, seasonal moments, limited bundles, flash promotions (short, rare).

## Lesson 10.6: Reviewing performance
Flows: open above 35%, click above 1%, revenue per recipient trend. Campaigns: about 35 to 40% open, about 1.3% click, unsubscribe under 0.3%. Fix the weakest step: subject line (opens), content and call to action (clicks), landing page (orders).

## Lesson 10.7: A/B testing framework
Hypothesis -> one variable (subject, send time, offer, layout) -> split -> enough recipients -> record result -> roll out.

## Lesson 10.8: Deliverability
Authenticate domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), warm up gradually (each send no more than about 1.5x the last, engaged first), suppress invalid addresses (typos like misspelled domains), sunset non-engagers, watch provider-specific bounce spikes, avoid spammy formatting in sale emails.

## Lesson 10.9: Platform costs
Email platforms often charge by profile count. Suppress or remove long-inactive profiles, archive old lists, compare alternatives if cost per profile outweighs value.

## Lesson 10.10: SMS
One SMS where email failed (welcome, checkout), sale launches and last chance. Register sender IDs where required (for example Australia). Respect quiet hours. Keep it short with one link.

## Lesson 10.11: WhatsApp and chat commerce
Useful for VIPs, launches and conversational selling where customers use it. Opt-in only, templates for marketing, quick replies for questions. Sales via chat can work for high-consideration products.

## Lesson 10.12: Loyalty
Only when repeat rate is above about 15 to 20%. Points or cashback aligned to margin, VIP tiers with early access, referral rewards. Measure repeat rate change, not points issued.

## Lesson 10.13: Sale email and SMS schedule
Hype (sign-up and teasers) -> launch (email + SMS) -> mid-sale (new reason) -> last chance (email + SMS) -> post-sale nurture for new buyers (thank you, how to use, results, small time-limited offer). Swap abandonment flows to sale versions at a scheduled time.
