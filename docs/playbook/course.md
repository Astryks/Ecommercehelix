# The Helix Course

**What it is:** a guided program on top of the Helix lessons. You work on one clear goal at a time. Each goal has a finish line you can measure, and it is split into short days of tasks. Tick a day's tasks and the next day opens. Hit the finish line and the next goal opens, with a small celebration.

**Where it lives:** the Today screen (Your goal, Today's tasks, Why this matters) and the [/course](https://ecommercehelix.com/course) path page. Every task links to the lesson step it comes from, with Do it for me and Guide me.

## How it works

1. **One goal at a time.** The goal is written in plain words with a finish line, for example "Get add-to-cart above 8%". A progress bar shows how close you are.
2. **Days of tasks.** Each goal runs over several days. A day has 1 task on light days and up to 5 on busy days. Each task is one action, with the result you should see, a time estimate and a link to the lesson step.
3. **The next day unlocks** as soon as the current day's tasks are ticked. You can read ahead, but you tick in order.
4. **Finish lines are checked from your numbers** where Helix can (sessions, add-to-cart rate, conversion rate, average order value, margin, sales and returning customers, from the numbers you enter or sync). Real days only: example data never counts. Where Helix cannot check (for example "Five flows live"), you confirm it yourself once the days are done. If a numbers-based goal has too little data, you can confirm it yourself too.
5. **Dated tasks slot in by themselves.** Sale prep from the seasonal calendar (Black Friday from 1 August, Christmas, Boxing Day, Mother's and Father's Day, EOFY) and tax dates (BAS, end of financial year, US tax dates) appear in Today's list when due within a week, at most two at a time, so the day never gets crowded. If several earlier steps of a plan have already passed (for example joining in October, after the August and September Black Friday steps), they show as one catch-up line instead of a pile of overdue tasks.
6. **Ticks are shared with the lessons.** Ticking a course task ticks the lesson step, and a step you ticked in a lesson counts in the course.

## Free and paid goals

The three numbers goals (and, on the Just starting track, getting the store ready) are free, with their lessons, the profit dashboard and daily numbers. Attract and Convert goals need Starter; Grow and Run goals need Growth. On a free plan the path shows every goal, and the goal card shows a short upgrade note when the free goals are done. Every lesson stays readable in Learn on every plan. The plan check is enforced on the server when a task is ticked or a goal is confirmed, not only in the page.

## The ladder

Goals follow the profit-first order. Every track starts the same way: **1. Set up your profit dashboard** (selling price, product cost, freight and duty, packaging, shipping, payment fees, subscriptions and marketing, so you see profit per order and per month), **2. Fill in your numbers 7 days in a row**, and **3. Learn the numbers that run your store** (break-even, target MER, how much to spend on marketing and your monthly goals). Then Attract (get the right visitors), Convert (turn visits into orders), Grow (keep more of every sale and bring buyers back), then run the business with a plan, a weekly rhythm, playbooks and a team. You choose a track:

| Track | Monthly sales | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Just starting | $0 to $10k | Launching, or a few sales a week |
| Growing | $10k to $80k | Regular sales, ready for more profit |
| Scaling | Over $80k | Needs systems and a team |

## Goals, days and tasks

Each goal's tasks are the steps of the lessons listed, in order. Days follow a light and busy rhythm, and no task is estimated at more than 20 minutes.

### Set up your profit dashboard

**Finish line:** Your price, product, freight, packaging, shipping, fees, subscriptions and marketing costs are saved in Profit setup.  
**Why this matters:** Sales feel good, but profit is what you keep. Five minutes entering your costs shows what one order and one month really leave you, and every other screen in Helix uses it.  
**Lessons:** 1.7, 1.8, 1.2 · **Days:** 5 (2, 3, 3, 2, 1 tasks, about 25, 25, 30, 15, 5 min)

### Fill in your numbers 7 days in a row

**Finish line:** 7 of the last 7 days are filled in with your real numbers.  
**Why this matters:** A daily 2-minute check catches a bad ad or a cost blow-out in one day instead of one month.  
**Lessons:** 1.3, 1.6 · **Days:** 6 (2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1 tasks, about 15, 15, 5, 5, 5, 15 min)

### Learn the numbers that run your store

**Finish line:** You know your break-even MER, your target MER and your monthly sales and profit goals, and they are saved in Helix.  
**Why this matters:** A handful of numbers tell you whether to push, hold or fix. Knowing your limits before you spend more on growth keeps every next goal profitable.  
**Lessons:** 1.1, 1.4, 1.5, 1.9 · **Days:** 6 (1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 1 tasks, about 15, 40, 40, 20, 40, 5 min)

### Get your store ready to take orders

**Finish line:** A real test order goes through checkout and lands in your inbox.  
**Why this matters:** Every visitor you pay for is wasted if the store is slow, unclear or cannot take a payment.  
**Lessons:** 22.1, 22.3, 22.5, 22.9, 22.11, 22.12, 22.16 · **Days:** 11 (1, 3, 4, 1, 5, 3, 4, 1, 3, 4, 2 tasks, about 15, 25, 40, 10, 55, 35, 35, 10, 40, 30, 20 min)

### Get your first 100 site visits a day

**Finish line:** An average of 100 sessions a day over the last 7 days.  
**Why this matters:** You need steady visitors before you can learn what makes people buy.  
**Lessons:** 7.14, 7.13, 4.2, 21.2, 29.3, 6.1, 6.2, 7.1, 7.4 · **Days:** 17 (1, 3, 4, 2, 4, 3, 4, 2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 1, 3, 3, 2, 3 tasks, about 5, 20, 35, 20, 40, 35, 35, 25, 20, 25, 20, 25, 15, 20, 45, 20, 25 min)

### Get 1,000 site visits a day while ads stay profitable

**Finish line:** 1,000 sessions a day on average over 7 days, with ad spend at or under your target MER.  
**Why this matters:** More of the right visitors is the fastest lever, but only if each sale still makes money.  
**Lessons:** 7.14, 7.13, 5.1, 5.7, 6.3, 6.12, 7.6, 19.12 · **Days:** 14 (1, 3, 4, 2, 4, 3, 3, 2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 1, 3 tasks, about 5, 20, 35, 20, 40, 20, 35, 35, 20, 25, 15, 35, 10, 35 min)

### Add 500 people to your email list

**Finish line:** 500 new subscribers since you started this goal (check your email tool).  
**Why this matters:** Your list is the one audience you own. Every subscriber can buy again without paying for another ad.  
**Lessons:** 10.1, 11.1, 11.3, 11.4 · **Days:** 6 (1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 2 tasks, about 5, 35, 30, 20, 60, 25 min)

### Get your first 10 orders from referrals and partners

**Finish line:** 10 orders came through a referral link, partner code or guest feature.  
**Why this matters:** A friend's word or a trusted brand's mention wins customers at a fraction of the cost of ads.  
**Lessons:** 29.4, 29.5, 29.6 · **Days:** 5 (1, 3, 4, 2, 2 tasks, about 5, 30, 40, 25, 15 min)

### Get add-to-cart above 8%

**Finish line:** 8% or more of sessions add something to the cart, over 7 days.  
**Why this matters:** If people do not add to cart, the product page is not convincing them. Fixing it lifts every channel at once.  
**Lessons:** 29.1, 3.2, 9.1, 9.2, 9.5 · **Days:** 8 (1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 3, 4, 1 tasks, about 5, 45, 45, 20, 45, 15, 40, 10 min)

### Get your conversion rate to 2.5%

**Finish line:** 2.5% or more of sessions become an order, over 7 days.  
**Why this matters:** Going from 1.5% to 2.5% conversion brings about two thirds more sales from the same ad spend.  
**Lessons:** 3.3, 3.5, 9.4, 22.12, 9.8, 9.16 · **Days:** 10 (1, 3, 4, 1, 5, 2, 4, 2, 2, 4 tasks, about 10, 35, 30, 20, 45, 15, 50, 25, 10, 35 min)

### Switch on the five core email flows

**Finish line:** Welcome, abandoned checkout, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back flows are all live.  
**Why this matters:** Flows sell while you sleep. Most stores leave 10% or more of revenue on the table without them.  
**Lessons:** 10.2, 10.3, 29.8, 29.7 · **Days:** 6 (1, 3, 4, 1, 5, 2 tasks, about 15, 45, 40, 10, 55, 15 min)

### Raise your average order by 10%

**Finish line:** Average order value is 10% higher than when you started this goal, over 7 days.  
**Why this matters:** A bigger order pays for the same ad click, so profit per order rises with no extra traffic.  
**Lessons:** 9.10, 3.6, 9.12 · **Days:** 5 (1, 3, 4, 1, 2 tasks, about 10, 25, 30, 5, 20 min)

### Reach $10k a month in sales

**Finish line:** $10,000 or more in sales over the last 30 days.  
**Why this matters:** At $10k a month you have proof people want what you sell, and enough data to grow on purpose.  
**Lessons:** 6.14, 13.1, 6.6, 3.8 · **Days:** 6 (1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 1 tasks, about 15, 30, 30, 20, 55, 10 min)

### Get net profit margin to 10%

**Finish line:** Net profit is 10% or more of sales over the last 30 days.  
**Why this matters:** Ten cents of every dollar kept is what lets you survive a bad month and fund the next stage.  
**Lessons:** 2.1, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5, 13.12 · **Days:** 9 (1, 2, 4, 2, 4, 3, 3, 2, 2 tasks, about 15, 25, 40, 10, 45, 20, 20, 10, 25 min)

### Get 25% of sales from returning customers

**Finish line:** A quarter of sales over 30 days come from people who bought before.  
**Why this matters:** Returning customers cost almost nothing to win back, so they are the most profitable sales you make.  
**Lessons:** 10.4, 10.12, 4.7, 3.10, 23.7 · **Days:** 8 (1, 2, 4, 2, 5, 3, 4, 2 tasks, about 20, 30, 30, 15, 45, 30, 40, 15 min)

### Reach a $1M-a-year pace with at least 10% profit

**Finish line:** $84,000 in sales over 30 days (about $1M a year) and a net margin of 10% or more.  
**Why this matters:** This is the jump from a busy shop to a real business that can pay a team and its owner.  
**Lessons:** 2.7, 7.10, 8.6, 15.1, 28.9 · **Days:** 7 (1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 2, 4 tasks, about 20, 40, 45, 15, 45, 10, 55 min)

### Grow sales 20% month on month while ads stay profitable

**Finish line:** Sales over the last 30 days are 20% higher than the 30 days before, with MER at or under target.  
**Why this matters:** Scaling only counts if profit scales with it. This proves you can add spend without losing money.  
**Lessons:** 2.7, 7.10, 15.2, 15.6 · **Days:** 5 (1, 3, 4, 2, 4 tasks, about 20, 40, 45, 15, 40 min)

### Set your 90-day plan

**Finish line:** One goal, three monthly checkpoints and up to three projects, written on one page.  
**Why this matters:** A clear plan stops you chasing every idea, so the next 90 days add up to real progress.  
**Lessons:** 29.15, 29.13, 29.9 · **Days:** 6 (2, 2, 3, 1, 2, 2 tasks, about 35, 30, 40, 5, 15, 20 min)

### Run a weekly numbers meeting

**Finish line:** Four weekly meetings held, each ending with one fix and an owner.  
**Why this matters:** Checking in every week gives you 52 chances a year to fix problems early.  
**Lessons:** 29.10, 20.10, 29.12, 29.14 · **Days:** 5 (1, 2, 4, 1, 3 tasks, about 5, 25, 40, 10, 30 min)

### Write playbooks for your three most repeated tasks

**Finish line:** Three playbooks exist and someone else has followed each one without help.  
**Why this matters:** Work that lives in your head only happens when you do it. Playbooks set you free.  
**Lessons:** 29.11, 28.2 · **Days:** 5 (1, 3, 4, 2, 1 tasks, about 5, 35, 45, 20, 10 min)

### Free up your first 10 hours a week

**Finish line:** Someone else now does at least 10 hours a week of work you used to do.  
**Why this matters:** Your time is the business's scarcest resource. Freeing it is how you work on growth, not just in it.  
**Lessons:** 28.1, 16.4, 28.6, 29.16 · **Days:** 6 (1, 3, 4, 2, 4, 3 tasks, about 5, 35, 35, 20, 35, 35 min)

## A sample day

Goal: Set up your profit dashboard, day 2:

- [ ] Open Profit setup in Helix. *You will have:* The waterfall on the right shows your profit per order and your break-even numbers. (5 min, Lesson 1.8 step 1)
- [ ] Enter your monthly costs: your Shopify or website plan, every app subscription, and any wages, rent or software. *You will have:* You can see your net profit for a month at that number of orders. (10 min, Lesson 1.8 step 2)
- [ ] Enter marketing per order (last month's ad spend divided by orders), tick "Use my monthly costs as fixed costs" and save. *You will have:* Profit setup is saved and your dashboard uses your real fixed costs. (10 min, Lesson 1.8 step 3)

## New lessons added for the course

Module 29 (Customers, partners and the weekly rhythm) fills gaps the course needed: customer words, the worry behind the purchase, repurposing content, referrals, partner brands, guest spots, a weekly story email, buying-question emails, leading numbers, a weekly numbers meeting, playbooks, owners, a 90-day plan, a quarterly reset, a one-page direction and a wins file. Five are Attract, three Convert and eight Grow.

Later additions: Lessons 1.7 to 1.9 (sales, costs and profit in plain English; setting up the profit dashboard; how much to spend on marketing) power the first and third goals. Lessons 3.10 (subscribe and save), 9.16 (when conversion suddenly drops), 13.12 (a smaller sale between the big ones), 23.7 (customer service that keeps customers) and 28.9 (selling to shops) fill topics the course was missing and are tasks in the conversion, margin, returning customer and $1M-a-year goals.

Lessons 7.14 (find your way around Ads Manager) and 7.13 (launch your first Meta ad in 30 minutes) open the first traffic goals.

## Where the code lives

- Goals, finish lines, day building and dated-task slotting: `src/lib/course-plan.ts` (pure, tested in `tests/course.test.ts`).
- Loading, advancing and seasonal tasks: `src/lib/course.ts`.
- Progress: the `CourseState` table (track, current goal, finished and confirmed goals) and the existing `TaskCompletion` and `LessonProgress` tables for ticks.
- Profit setup: `src/lib/profit-setup.ts` (waterfall and marketing guide), the `ProfitSetup` table and `src/app/dashboard/profit`.
- Screens: `src/app/course` (path and goal day view) and the goal card on Today (`src/components/course`).
