# Module 18: Choosing your tool stack

**Outcome:** the right tools for your stage, without app bloat.

---

## Lesson 18.1: The stack by need and stage

| Need | Tool category | When to add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviews | Review and UGC app with photo reviews and requests | From day one |
| Email capture | Pop-up and form builder | From day one |
| Email and SMS | Email platform with flows and segments; SMS provider | From day one (email), Stage 1+ (SMS) |
| Upsells and bundles | Cart upsell, bundle builder, post-purchase offers | Stage 1+ |
| Testing | A/B testing platform | When traffic supports tests (thousands of sessions per variant) |
| Behaviour analytics | Heatmaps and session recordings (free options exist) | From day one |
| Attribution | Multi-touch or incrementality tool | Stage 2+ with several channels |
| Creative analytics | Tool that groups ads by concept and hook | Stage 2+ |
| Shoppable video | Product page and homepage video | Stage 2+ |
| Returns | Returns and exchanges portal | When returns are meaningful |
| Support | Help desk with automations or AI assistant | Stage 1+ |
| Fulfilment | 3PL (local or international) | When packing takes over your week |
| Shipping | Multi-carrier rates, labels | Early |
| Payments and FX | Multi-currency accounts | When selling internationally |
| International tax | Duty and tax calculation | When exporting |
| Loyalty | Points, VIP or cashback | Repeat rate above 15 to 20% |
| Creator gifting | Creator discovery and gifting platform | Stage 1+ |
| Packaging | Sustainable packaging supplier | Early |

## Lesson 18.2: Rules for adding a tool
1. Name the metric it should move.
2. Check speed impact and monthly cost as % of revenue.
3. Trial with a clear test period.
4. Remove it if the metric did not move.
