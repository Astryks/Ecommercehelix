# Module 18: Choosing your tool stack

**Outcome:** the right tools for where your store is today, without paying for apps you do not need.


<!-- plain:start -->
> **In plain words**
>
> **What it is:** Which apps and tools you need at each stage, and which to skip.
>
> **Why it matters:** Too many apps slow your store and cost money. Fewer, better tools win.
>
> **Do this:**
>
> 1. List every app you pay for.
> 2. Remove ones you do not use each week.
> 3. Add a tool only when it solves a problem you have today.
>
> **Words to know:**
>
> - **Landing page:** The page someone lands on after clicking an ad.
>
> All words are explained in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Lesson 18.1: The stack by need and stage

Stages used below:

- **Day one:** you are just starting.
- **Stage 1:** you have steady sales every week.
- **Stage 2:** you sell through several channels (for example Meta, Google and email) and spend a meaningful amount on ads.

| Need | Type of tool | When to add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviews | A reviews app with photo reviews and automatic review requests | Day one |
| Email sign-ups | A pop-up and form builder | Day one |
| Email and SMS | An email platform with flows and segments; an SMS provider | Email on day one. SMS from Stage 1. |
| Bigger orders | Cart upsells, a bundle builder, offers after purchase | Stage 1 |
| Testing | An A/B testing app | When you have enough visitors (thousands of sessions for each version) |
| Seeing how people use your site | Heatmaps and session recordings (free options exist) | Day one |
| Attribution (which channel gets credit) | A multi-touch or incrementality tool | Stage 2, with several channels |
| Ad analysis | A tool that groups ads by idea and hook | Stage 2 |
| Shoppable video | Video on product pages and the homepage that people can shop from | Stage 2 |
| Returns | A returns and exchanges portal | When returns become a real job |
| Customer support | A help desk with automations or an AI assistant | Stage 1 |
| Fulfilment | A 3PL (a company that stores, packs and ships for you), local or overseas | When packing takes over your week |
| Shipping | Rates from several carriers, and label printing | Early |
| Payments in other currencies | Multi-currency bank accounts | When you sell overseas |
| International tax | Duty and tax calculation | When you ship to other countries |
| Loyalty | Points, VIP levels or cashback | When more than 15 to 20% of customers buy again |
| Sending free product to creators | A creator search and gifting platform | Stage 1 |
| Packaging | A sustainable packaging supplier | Early |

## Lesson 18.2: Rules for adding a tool

Follow these four steps before you add any app.

1. **Name the number it should improve.** For example "email sign-ups per 100 visitors".
2. **Check the cost.** How much does it slow your site? What does it cost each month, as a share of sales?
3. **Trial it for a set time,** for example 30 days.
4. **Remove it if the number did not improve.**
