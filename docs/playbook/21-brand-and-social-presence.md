# Module 21: Brand and social presence

**Outcome:** your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook profiles look like a brand people can trust, match what your ads promise, and turn curious ad viewers into followers, subscribers and buyers. You have a posting schedule you can keep and a simple way to reach more people for free.

## Lesson 21.1: Why your profile is part of every ad
<!-- stage:attract -->

Many people who see an ad do not click it. They tap your name to check you out first.

- If they find three old posts, no reviews and a broken link, they leave.
- A strong profile makes every ad dollar work harder, for free.
- It is also the first place partners, creators and journalists look.

Think of each profile as a mini landing page with three jobs:

1. **Prove you are real.**
2. **Show what you sell.**
3. **Give the next step.**

## Lesson 21.2: Profile checklist (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
<!-- stage:attract -->

Go through each item on every platform.

| Item | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Handle and name | The same handle on every platform. The name field includes a word people search ("Linen clothing for hot summers"), not just the brand. |
| Profile photo | Your logo or brand mark, readable at a tiny size, the same on every platform. |
| Bio, line 1 | What you sell and who it is for. |
| Bio, line 2 | Your proof or what makes you different (rating, number of customers, where it is made, guarantee). |
| Bio, line 3 | A reason to tap now (new release, free shipping amount, gift guide). |
| Link | Goes to a fast mobile page with 3 to 5 clear options: best sellers, new arrivals, offer sign-up, reviews. Add UTM tags so you can track visits. |
| Category and contact | A business account with a category, email and shop buttons turned on. |
| Shop | Product catalog connected so you can tag products in posts (where available). |
| Highlights (Instagram) | 4 to 7 saved story groups with simple matching covers: Reviews, Best sellers, How to use, Shipping and returns, Behind the scenes, Press, FAQ. |
| Pinned posts | 3 pinned: your best-performing product video, your strongest proof (reviews or a customer story), and your brand story or current offer. |
| Recent posts | Something posted in the last 7 days. No gaps of months. |
| Comments | Replied to within a day. Answer questions publicly so others see the answer. |
| Facebook page | Same bio and link, a current cover image, reviews managed, and the page connected to your ad account. |
| TikTok | Bio link turned on (not every account can yet), playlists for series, pinned videos. |

**Match your ads:** the product, offer and look in your ads should also be on your profile. If your ad says "free shipping this week" and your profile does not mention it, trust drops.

## Lesson 21.3: What to post: content pillars for social
<!-- stage:attract -->

Content pillars are the topics you always post about. Use these four (they build on [Lesson 4.4](04-brand-and-content.md#lesson-44-content-pillars-by-channel)):

1. **Proof:** reviews, before and after (within platform rules), customer photos, press, numbers.
2. **Education:** how to use it, how to choose, myths, care guides, comparisons.
3. **Story:** the founder, behind the scenes, how the product is made, the team, your values.
4. **Product and offer:** launches, restocks, bundles, gift guides, sales.

**A rough mix:** 40% education and proof, 30% story and community, 30% product and offer. Social feeds punish accounts that only sell.

**Ideas that never run out:**

1. Customer reviews, and comments on your posts.
2. Support questions. Each one can be a video: "You asked, we answered."
3. Negative comments, answered calmly and helpfully. These often reach lots of people.
4. Series ("Day 8 of packing every order myself", "Customer outfit of the week"). They give people a reason to come back.

## Lesson 21.4: Posting cadence you can keep
<!-- stage:attract -->

Cadence means how often you post.

| Stage | Instagram | TikTok | Facebook | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Just starting | 3 posts a week | 3 to 5 a week | Share your Instagram posts | Most days |
| Growing | 4 to 5 a week (mostly Reels) | 1 a day | 3 a week | Every day |
| Established | Every day | 1 to 3 a day | Every day | Every day, plus a broadcast channel |

**Rules:**

1. **Being consistent beats posting a lot.** Pick a schedule you can keep for 12 weeks.
2. **Batch your work.** Film once a week, then schedule the whole week.
3. **Make every post good enough to run as an ad later.** Free posts are your free testing lab. The winners become ads, partnership ads or Spark ads.
4. **Put most of the value in the first quarter of the video.** Say the key points twice.

## Lesson 21.5: Growing organic reach
<!-- stage:attract -->

Organic reach means people who see your posts without you paying.

1. **Trial reels and test posts.** Where the platform lets you show a reel to non-followers first, use it to test hooks without cluttering your feed. Check after 24 hours and share the winners with everyone.
2. **Social search.** People search Instagram and TikTok like Google. Say your keywords out loud in the video, and put them in on-screen text and captions ("linen shirt for hot weather"). The platforms read all of it.
3. **Hooks.** Use the same rules as ads ([Lesson 19.8](19-creators-ugc-and-video-ads.md#lesson-198-hooks-that-win-the-first-three-seconds)). Remake the first three seconds of your best free posts and post them again.
4. **Collaborations.** Collab posts with creators and partner brands show your content to their audience too.
5. **Reply fast.** Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting. It tells the platform people care about the post.
6. **Broadcast channels and close groups.** A channel or group for your keenest fans, with early access, polls and behind the scenes.
7. **Be helpful off the platform.** Answer questions in forums and communities where your customers ask for recommendations (for example Reddit threads that show up in Google). Be helpful, say who you are, and never spam.

## Lesson 21.6: Social proof and reviews on social
<!-- stage:convert -->

1. Turn reviews into Instagram highlights, carousels and videos where you read them out.
2. Ask happy customers to tag you. Reshare their posts to Stories (with permission) and save them to a highlight.
3. Keep shop ratings and seller feedback healthy where you sell through social shops. Low scores can limit your reach and ad results there.
4. Reply to bad reviews publicly and kindly, then fix the problem privately. Future customers judge your reply more than the complaint.

## Lesson 21.7: Community, not just audience
<!-- stage:attract -->

People buy from brands they feel part of.

1. Let customers help decide things (polls on colours, names or flavours).
2. Feature customers as the faces of a collection.
3. Run small events, meetups or live sessions.
4. Thank and reward your biggest fans: early access, gifts, a VIP group.
5. Share the feedback you acted on ("You asked for bigger sizes. Here they are.").

## Lesson 21.8: Social shops
<!-- stage:attract -->

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok shops let people buy without leaving the app. Before you turn one on:

1. **Connect a clean catalog:** one price per product and correct stock levels.
2. **State shipping times and returns clearly.** Slow shipping hurts your shop score.
3. **Check what works in the in-app checkout.** Some store features, like offers on the thank-you page, will not work there.
4. **Check seller fees,** and how in-app orders flow into your store and your email platform.

See also [Lesson 12.6 TikTok Shop](12-channels-tiktok-pinterest-seo.md#lesson-126-tiktok-shop) and [Lesson 12.10 Instagram shopping](12-channels-tiktok-pinterest-seo.md#lesson-1210-instagram-shopping).

## Lesson 21.9: The social presence audit (what Helix checks)
<!-- stage:attract -->

Helix checks your social profiles as part of its regular audit. It uses public information, plus account insights once you connect.

| Check | Helix flags it when |
| --- | --- |
| Bio says what you sell | No product or category word in the name or bio |
| Link works on mobile | The link is missing, broken or slow, or goes to a general home page with no UTM tags |
| Recent activity | No post in the last 14 days |
| How often you post | Fewer than 2 posts a week over the last 4 weeks |
| Highlights | Fewer than 3, or no reviews or shipping highlight |
| Pinned posts | None pinned, or pinned posts older than 6 months |
| Social proof | No review or customer content in the last 10 posts |
| Matches your ads | Products or offers in your live ads are not visible on the profile |
| Comment care | Questions left unanswered for more than 48 hours |
| Same name everywhere | Different handles or names across platforms |
| Shop | Catalog connected but no products tagged in posts, or the shop rating is dropping |

## Self-check

1. What three jobs does a profile do?
2. What should your three pinned posts be?
3. Why say your keywords out loud in videos?
4. Your ad promotes a bundle that is nowhere on your profile. Why does it matter?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Prove you are real, show what you sell, give the next step.
2. Your best-performing product video, your strongest proof, and your brand story or current offer.
3. Platforms read speech, captions and on-screen text for social search and recommendations.
4. Many people check your profile before buying. A mismatch lowers trust and sales.
