# Module 21: Brand and social presence

**Outcome:** your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook profiles look like a brand people can trust, match what your ads promise, and turn curious ad viewers into followers, subscribers and buyers. You have a posting rhythm you can sustain and a simple way to grow organic reach.

## Lesson 21.1: Why your profile is part of every ad

Many people who see an ad do not click it. They tap your name to check you out first. If they land on a profile with three old posts, no reviews and a broken link, they leave. A strong profile is a free conversion booster for every dollar you spend on ads, and it is the first place partners, creators and press look.

Think of each profile as a mini landing page with one job: **prove you are real, show what you sell, and give the next step.**

## Lesson 21.2: Profile checklist (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)

| Element | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Handle and name | Same handle on every platform. Name field includes a searchable keyword ("Linen clothing for hot summers"), not just the brand |
| Profile photo | Logo or brand mark readable at tiny size, consistent across platforms |
| Bio, line 1 | What you sell and who it is for |
| Bio, line 2 | Your proof or difference (rating, customers, made in, guarantee) |
| Bio, line 3 | A reason to tap now (new drop, free shipping threshold, gift guide) |
| Link | Goes to a fast mobile page with 3 to 5 clear options: shop best sellers, new arrivals, offer sign-up, reviews. Track with UTMs |
| Category and contact | Business account with category, email and shop buttons on |
| Shop | Product catalogue connected so posts can be tagged (where available) |
| Highlights (Instagram) | 4 to 7 with simple matching covers: Reviews, Best sellers, How to use, Shipping and returns, Behind the scenes, Press, FAQ |
| Pinned posts | 3 pinned: your best-performing product video, your strongest proof (reviews or a customer story), your brand story or current offer |
| Recent grid | Posted in the last 7 days. No gaps of months |
| Comments | Replied to within a day. Questions answered publicly so others see it |
| Facebook page | Same bio and link, cover image current, reviews or recommendations managed, page connected to the ad account |
| TikTok | Bio link enabled (eligibility varies), playlists for series, pinned videos |

**Consistency with ads:** the product, offer and look in your ads should appear on your profile. If your ad says "free shipping this week" and your profile has no mention of it, trust drops.

## Lesson 21.3: What to post: content pillars for social

Build around four pillars (adapt from [Lesson 4.4](04-brand-and-content.md#lesson-44-content-pillars-by-channel)):

1. **Proof:** reviews, before and after (within policy), customer photos, press, numbers.
2. **Education:** how to use, how to choose, myths, care guides, comparisons.
3. **Story:** founder, behind the scenes, making the product, team, values.
4. **Product and offer:** launches, restocks, bundles, gift guides, sales.

Rough mix: 40% education and proof, 30% story and community, 30% product and offer. Social feeds punish pure selling.

Content sources that never run dry:

- customer reviews and the comments on your posts
- support questions (each one is a video: "You asked, we answered")
- negative comments, answered calmly and helpfully (these often get strong reach)
- series formats ("Day 8 of packing every order myself", "Customer outfit of the week") that give people a reason to come back

## Lesson 21.4: Posting cadence you can keep

| Stage | Instagram | TikTok | Facebook | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Just starting | 3 posts a week | 3 to 5 a week | Share Instagram posts | Most days |
| Growing | 4 to 5 a week (mostly Reels) | 1 a day | 3 a week | Daily |
| Established | Daily | 1 to 3 a day | Daily | Daily, plus broadcast channel |

Rules:

- Consistency beats volume. Pick a rhythm you can hold for 12 weeks.
- Batch: film once a week, schedule the week.
- Every post should be good enough to run as an ad later. Organic is your free creative test lab: winners become ads, partnership ads or Spark ads.
- Put 80% of the value in the first quarter of the video. Say key points twice.

## Lesson 21.5: Growing organic reach

- **Trial reels and test posts:** where the platform lets you show a reel to non-followers first, use it to test hooks without hurting your feed. Check results after 24 hours and post the winners to everyone.
- **Social search:** people search Instagram and TikTok like a search engine. Say your keywords out loud in the video, put them in on-screen text and captions ("linen shirt for hot weather"). Platforms read all of it.
- **Hooks:** the same rules as ads ([Lesson 19.8](19-creators-ugc-and-video-ads.md#lesson-198-hooks-that-win-the-first-three-seconds)). Iterate the first three seconds of your best organic posts and repost.
- **Collaborations:** collab posts with creators and partner brands show your content to their audience too.
- **Engagement windows:** reply to every comment in the first hour after posting; it signals relevance.
- **Broadcast channels and close communities:** a broadcast channel or group for your most engaged fans gives early access, polls and behind the scenes.
- **Off-platform presence:** answer questions in forums and communities where your customers ask for recommendations (for example Reddit threads that rank in search). Be helpful, disclose who you are, never spam.

## Lesson 21.6: Social proof and reviews on social

- Turn reviews into highlights, carousels and video reads.
- Ask happy customers to tag you; reshare their posts (with permission) to Stories and save them to a highlight.
- Keep shop ratings and seller feedback healthy where you sell through social shops. Low feedback scores can limit your reach and ad performance there.
- Respond to bad reviews publicly and kindly, then fix the issue privately. Future customers judge the reply more than the complaint.

## Lesson 21.7: Community, not just audience

People buy from brands they feel part of:

- invite customers into product decisions (polls on colours, names, flavours)
- feature customers as the faces of a collection
- run small events, meetups or live sessions
- thank and reward your loudest fans (early access, gifts, a VIP group)
- share the feedback you acted on ("You asked for bigger sizes. Here they are.")

## Lesson 21.8: Social shops

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok shops let people buy without leaving the app. Before turning one on:

- connect a clean catalogue (one price per product, correct stock)
- check your shipping times and returns are stated clearly; slow fulfilment hurts your shop score
- decide whether discounts and bundles will work inside the in-app checkout (some store features, like thank-you page upsells, will not)
- watch seller fees and how in-app orders flow into your store and email platform

See also [Lesson 12.6 TikTok Shop](12-channels-tiktok-pinterest-seo.md#lesson-126-tiktok-shop) and [Lesson 12.10 Instagram shopping](12-channels-tiktok-pinterest-seo.md#lesson-1210-instagram-shopping).

## Lesson 21.9: The social presence audit (what Helix checks)

Helix's proactive audit includes your social profiles (public data, plus account insights once connected):

| Check | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| Bio says what you sell | No product or category keyword in name or bio |
| Link works and is mobile friendly | Link missing, broken, slow, or goes to a generic home page with no UTMs |
| Recent activity | No post in the last 14 days |
| Cadence | Fewer than 2 posts a week over the last 4 weeks |
| Highlights | Fewer than 3, or no reviews or shipping highlight |
| Pinned posts | None pinned, or pinned posts older than 6 months |
| Social proof | No review or customer content in the last 10 posts |
| Ad consistency | Products or offers in live ads not visible on the profile |
| Comment care | Unanswered questions older than 48 hours |
| Handle consistency | Different handles or names across platforms |
| Shop | Catalogue connected but not tagged in posts, or shop rating dropping |

## Self-check

1. What three jobs does a profile do?
2. What should your three pinned posts be?
3. Why say your keywords out loud in videos?
4. Your ad promotes a bundle that is nowhere on your profile. Why does it matter?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Prove you are real, show what you sell, give the next step.
2. Best-performing product video, strongest proof, brand story or current offer.
3. Platforms read speech, captions and on-screen text for social search and recommendations.
4. Many people check your profile before buying. A mismatch lowers trust and conversion.
