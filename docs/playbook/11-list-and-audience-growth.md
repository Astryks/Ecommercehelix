# Module 11: List and audience growth

**Outcome:** a growing owned audience that makes every launch and sale cheaper.

---

## Lesson 11.1: Why build the list
Each engaged subscriber earns a small amount every month for as long as they stay engaged. Acquiring emails when ad costs are low and selling to them when costs are high is one of the most reliable growth levers.

## Lesson 11.2: Competition types
| Type | How it works | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweepstakes | One big prize, draw at the end | Cheap per entry, big reach | Lower-intent entrants, delayed purchases |
| Instant win | Many small prizes, win on entry | Can run for months, more immediate buying | Needs more prizes, higher cost per email |
| Collaboration giveaway | Several aligned brands pool prizes | Shared reach and audiences | Coordination, mixed audiences |
| Social comp | Like, follow, tag | Engagement and reach spike | Low list value |

Prize should appeal only to your customer (your products or a related experience), not a generic gadget.

## Lesson 11.3: Running a competition
1. Choose type and prize. Check local promotion rules.
2. Build an embedded form on a store page and a pop-up variant.
3. Create assets: prize image, video, entry steps.
4. Ads: lead or sales-to-page campaigns to broad audiences.
5. Welcome flow for entrants with a small offer.
6. After the draw: announce, then a consolation offer.
7. Analyse: cost per email, entrant purchase rate over 90 days.

## Lesson 11.4: Fill the funnel before peaks
Start 6 to 10 weeks before big sales. Pair with hype pages and early-access lists.

## Lesson 11.5: Social growth tactics
Boost top organic Reels, sell through chat replies, keep comment spam under control with filters and blocked words, and collaborate with complementary brands.
