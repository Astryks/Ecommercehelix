# Module 11: List and audience growth

**Outcome:** a growing audience you own (email, SMS and followers) that makes every launch and sale cheaper.

**Related SOPs:** [14 List growth and campaigns](../sops/14-list-growth-and-campaigns.md)

## Lesson 11.1: Why build the list
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Your email and SMS list is an audience you own and can reach for free, any time. Each engaged subscriber earns a little every month. Collecting emails when ads are cheap and selling when ads are expensive is one of the most reliable ways to grow.

**Step 1.** Check your list size and the share engaged in the last 90 days in your email app.

**Expected result:** You know your owned audience size today.

**Step 2.** Set a list growth target for the next quiet months, for example +2,000 subscribers before October.

**Expected result:** You have a written target with a date.

<!-- do:goals -->

**Step 3.** Plan one list-building push (form upgrade, competition or lead ads) in a month when ads are cheap.

**Expected result:** The push is on your calendar.

<!-- do:calendar -->

## Lesson 11.2: Competition types
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** A competition (giveaway) can add thousands of subscribers quickly. The type and the prize decide whether you get future buyers or prize hunters.

**Step 1.** Read the four competition types in the table below and pick one.

**Expected result:** You have chosen a type.

**Step 2.** Choose a prize only your kind of customer wants: your own products or an experience linked to them.

**Expected result:** The prize will attract future buyers, not prize hunters.

**Watch out:** Never offer a general gadget like a phone or a gift card.

### Good to know

| Type | How it works | Good | Not so good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweepstakes | One big prize, drawn at the end | Cheap per entry, reaches lots of people | Many entrants are not keen buyers; purchases come later |
| Instant win | Many small prizes; people find out right away if they won | Can run for months; people buy sooner | Needs more prizes, so each email costs more |
| Collaboration giveaway | Several brands with similar customers share one prize pool | Each brand reaches the others' audiences | Takes coordination; audiences can be mixed |
| Social competition | Like, follow and tag a friend to enter | Big boost in likes and reach | Adds little to your email list |

## Lesson 11.3: Running a competition
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Running a competition well is a seven-part job, from rules to measuring who buys. Skipping the follow-up flow wastes most of the value.

**Step 1.** Pick the type and prize, and check your local promotion rules (some places need a permit).

**Expected result:** You know the competition is legal where you run it.

**Step 2.** Build the entry form on a store page and as a pop-up.

**Expected result:** People can enter on your site in two ways.

**Step 3.** Make the content: a prize image, a short video and clear steps to enter.

**Expected result:** The creative is ready.

**Step 4.** Run ads to broad audiences with a Leads campaign, or a Sales campaign sending people to the entry page.

**Expected result:** Ads are live and entries are coming in.

<!-- do:meta-draft -->

**Step 5.** Set up a welcome flow for entrants with a small offer.

**Expected result:** Every entrant gets a welcome series.

<!-- do:flow-welcome -->

**Step 6.** After the draw, announce the winner and send everyone else a "sorry you didn't win" offer.

**Expected result:** Non-winners get a reason to buy.

**Step 7.** Measure cost per email and how many entrants buy within 90 days.

**Expected result:** You know whether to run another one.

## Lesson 11.4: Fill the funnel before peaks
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** List growth pays off most just before big sales, when ads get expensive. Starting 6 to 10 weeks early fills the funnel while attention is still cheap.

**Step 1.** Mark the date 6 to 10 weeks before your next big sale and start a list-growth push then.

**Expected result:** The push start date is in your calendar.

<!-- do:bfcm -->

**Step 2.** Build an excitement page teasing the sale with an early-access sign-up.

**Expected result:** Visitors can join an early-access list.

## Lesson 11.5: Social growth tactics
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** Social growth tactics add followers and customers alongside your list. Four simple moves cover most of it.

**Step 1.** Put a small budget (for example $5 to $10 a day) behind your best free Reels.

**Expected result:** Your best organic content reaches new people.

**Step 2.** Reply to direct messages with helpful answers and product links.

**Expected result:** DMs turn into sales.

**Step 3.** Turn on comment filters and blocked words, and team up with a brand whose products go well with yours.

**Expected result:** Comments stay clean and you reach a partner's audience.
