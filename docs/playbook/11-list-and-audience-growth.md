# Module 11: List and audience growth

**Outcome:** a growing audience you own (email, SMS and followers) that makes every launch and sale cheaper.

**Related SOPs:** [14 List growth and campaigns](../sops/14-list-growth-and-campaigns.md)

## Lesson 11.1: Why build the list

Your email and SMS list is an audience you own. You can reach it for free, any time.

- Each engaged subscriber earns you a little every month, for as long as they stay interested.
- **The big idea:** collect emails when ads are cheap (for example in quiet months). Then sell to those people when ads are expensive (for example around Black Friday). This is one of the most reliable ways to grow.

## Lesson 11.2: Competition types

A competition (giveaway) can add thousands of subscribers quickly. There are four kinds.

| Type | How it works | Good | Not so good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweepstakes | One big prize, drawn at the end | Cheap per entry, reaches lots of people | Many entrants are not keen buyers; purchases come later |
| Instant win | Many small prizes; people find out right away if they won | Can run for months; people buy sooner | Needs more prizes, so each email costs more |
| Collaboration giveaway | Several brands with similar customers share one prize pool | Each brand reaches the others' audiences | Takes coordination; audiences can be mixed |
| Social competition | Like, follow and tag a friend to enter | Big boost in likes and reach | Adds little to your email list |

**Decision rule:** choose a prize that only your kind of customer wants: your own products, or an experience linked to them. Never a general gadget like a phone or a gift card, which attracts prize hunters.

## Lesson 11.3: Running a competition

1. **Pick the type and the prize.** Check your local rules for promotions (some places need a permit).
2. **Build the entry form.** Put one on a page in your store, and make a pop-up version too.
3. **Make the content:** an image of the prize, a short video, and clear steps to enter.
4. **Run ads** to broad audiences. Use a Leads campaign, or a Sales campaign that sends people to the entry page.
5. **Set up a welcome flow** for entrants, with a small offer.
6. **After the draw:** announce the winner. Then send everyone else a "sorry you didn't win" offer.
7. **Measure the result:** cost per email, and how many entrants buy within 90 days.

## Lesson 11.4: Fill the funnel before peaks

Start growing your list 6 to 10 weeks before a big sale. Combine it with an excitement page (a page teasing the sale) and an early-access list.

## Lesson 11.5: Social growth tactics

1. Put a small ad budget behind your best free Reels (boost them).
2. Sell through replies in direct messages.
3. Keep spam out of your comments with filters and blocked words.
4. Team up with brands that sell things that go well with yours.
