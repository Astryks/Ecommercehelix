# Module 8: Google ads in practice

**Outcome:** a clean Google setup that captures demand profitably and that you can manage weekly.

**Related SOP:** [09](../sops/09-google-shopping-pmax-search.md)

---

## Lesson 8.1: Foundations
- [ ] Ads account owned by you (bring it in-house if an agency owns it).
- [ ] Store connected to Google (sales channel app) and Merchant Center.
- [ ] Conversion tracking: one primary purchase conversion, enhanced conversions on. Check monthly that nothing else became primary.
- [ ] Analytics (GA4): events, ecommerce reports, audiences.
- [ ] Audiences: site visitors, purchasers, email list, store-platform segments.
- [ ] Account settings reviewed (auto-apply recommendations off unless you choose them).
- [ ] Naming convention applied.

## Lesson 8.2: Product data
Titles front-load search terms (brand, type, key attribute). Clean main images, correct prices and shipping, GTINs where available, custom labels (margin tier, best seller, season). Fix every Merchant Center error. Organise products into groups you will bid on differently.

## Lesson 8.3: Campaign structures
- **Small accounts:** brand search + one feed-only PMax or Shopping campaign.
- **Growing:** add non-brand search for top terms, PMax split by hero products or margin tiers.
- **Larger:** new-customer focused campaigns, competitor search, Demand Gen, country splits.
- **PMax vs standard Shopping:** PMax reaches across Google; standard Shopping gives query control. Many stores run feed-only PMax with brand exclusions plus brand search.

## Lesson 8.4: Keyword research
Start with commercial and transactional terms (buy, best, price, product type + attribute). Check volume and competition. Group by theme. Build small ad groups (up to 5 keywords). Maintain shared negative lists.

## Lesson 8.5: Writing search ads
- Headlines: product + benefit, offer, proof (reviews, years), urgency, brand. Use up to 15; pin 1 to 2.
- Descriptions: reasons why, shipping and returns, call to action.
- Assets: sitelinks to best collections, callouts (free shipping, returns), structured snippets, images.

## Lesson 8.6: Target ROAS
Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ (1 - VCR). Target ROAS = break-even adjusted for profit you keep and for new-customer value. Change targets in modest steps. Expect bid strategy updates from Google; re-check after them.

## Lesson 8.7: New customer acquisition
Use customer lists to tell Google who existing customers are. Choose whether campaigns bid higher for new customers or only target new customers. Accept lower ROAS on new-customer campaigns if lifetime value supports it.

## Lesson 8.8: Weekly optimisation
- [ ] Search terms: add negatives, promote converters.
- [ ] PMax insights: which products, searches and assets perform.
- [ ] Budget-limited campaigns that hit target: raise budget.
- [ ] Brand vs non-brand split.
- [ ] Merchant Center diagnostics.

## Lesson 8.9: Demand Gen and YouTube
Short-form video and image ads across YouTube, Discover and Gmail. Reuse your best social videos in vertical and horizontal. Judge with view-through and platform-comparable metrics plus total MER.

## Lesson 8.10: Seasonality and sales
- Use a temporary seasonality adjustment for the first 1 to 2 days of a big sale (about +50% expected conversion rate), then remove it.
- Promotion assets and sale prices in the feed.
- Pre-sale: raise budgets gradually before the event so campaigns are not starved.

## Lesson 8.11: AI-driven changes in 2026
AI Max for Search and Shopping, AI-generated text, final URL expansion and AI Mode placements: review auto-upgraded campaigns, check generated copy and landing pages, keep brand exclusions, increase negative keyword reviews.
