## Lesson 28.1: Set up AI helpers for daily work
<!-- stage:grow -->

**Why this matters:** AI assistants (chat AI tools) work much better when they know about your business. A project with your context turns generic answers into useful ones.

**Step 1.** Create a project in your AI assistant. Most chat AI tools have a project (or similar) space where you can add files and instructions. Before you upload anything, check the tool's data settings and turn off using your chats for training if you can.

**Expected result:** You have an empty project for your store.

**Step 2.** Upload your brand core (Module 3), customer profiles, offers and costs (Module 1). Add your SOPs and past winning ads and emails.

**Expected result:** The project holds your business context.

**Step 3.** Update the files whenever something changes.

**Expected result:** Answers stay current with your business.

## Lesson 28.2: High-value uses
<!-- stage:grow -->

**Why this matters:** A handful of jobs save the most time with AI. Always read and edit what AI writes before you use it.

**Step 1.** Use AI for ads. It can write hooks, scripts, briefs and new ad text. It can lay out still images, resize, and find which parts of your ads win.

**Expected result:** Your next ad batch is drafted faster.

**Step 2.** Use AI for email: campaign drafts, subject lines and flow emails.

**Expected result:** Email drafts take minutes, not hours.

**Step 3.** Use AI for the website. It can write section text, FAQs and landing page drafts, check pages, and dig into your data when conversion drops.

**Expected result:** Site copy and audits are quicker.

**Step 4.** Use AI for operations: ad account naming, search term reviews, support replies, invoice entry and stock reports.

**Expected result:** Admin work shrinks.

**Step 5.** Set up an AI helper for "where is my order?" and common questions. Make sure it hands anything sensitive to a person.

**Expected result:** Routine questions are answered instantly.

## Lesson 28.3: Connectors and agents
<!-- stage:grow -->

**Why this matters:** Connectors let AI read your store, ad and email data, and agents can take steps for you. Any action that changes something must be approved by a person.

**Step 1.** Connect AI only through official integrations that respect platform limits.

**Expected result:** AI can read your data without risking a policy flag.

**Watch out:** Unofficial automation tools on ad accounts can trigger policy flags or bans.

**Step 2.** Set every write action (anything that changes something) to need your approval.

**Expected result:** Nothing changes in your accounts without your click.

## Lesson 28.4: Safety rules
<!-- stage:grow -->

**Why this matters:** AI is powerful and makes mistakes. A few rules keep you, your customers and your brand safe.

**Step 1.** Never paste passwords, access tokens or card details into an AI chat. Do not paste customers' names, emails or addresses into a tool unless your plan with that tool says it keeps business data private.

**Expected result:** Your logins and your customers' details stay private.

**Step 2.** Check every claim AI writes, especially health and before-and-after claims.

**Expected result:** Nothing false goes out under your name.

**Step 3.** Label AI-made people and voices where the rules say you must. If someone fakes your content or face, report it to the platform with proof.

**Expected result:** You follow the rules and protect your brand.

**Step 4.** Treat AI as a drafter, not a decider. Let it write and suggest, but you approve anything that spends money, changes an ad or goes to customers.

**Expected result:** No change reaches your accounts or customers without your OK.
