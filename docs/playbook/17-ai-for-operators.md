# Module 17: AI for e-commerce operators

**Outcome:** you use AI to save hours each week safely, without handing it the keys.

---

## Lesson 17.1: An AI operating system
Give your AI assistant a project with context: brand core, customer profiles, offers, cost drivers, SOPs, past winners. Better context gives better output.

## Lesson 17.2: High-value uses
- Creative: hooks, scripts, briefs, copy variants, static layouts, resizing, analysing which creative elements win.
- Email: campaign drafts, subject lines, flow copy.
- Website: section copy, FAQs, landing page drafts, audits, conversion investigations from data exports.
- Ads: naming, batch building, search term analysis, account audits.
- Operations: support replies, invoice entry, stock reports.
- Customer support agents: answer order status and FAQs, hand over to humans for anything sensitive.

## Lesson 17.3: Connectors and agents
Connectors let AI read store, ad and email data. Agents can take steps. Keep humans approving every write action. Third-party automations on ad accounts can trigger policy flags or bans; use official, rate-limited integrations.

## Lesson 17.4: Safety rules
- Never paste passwords or tokens into chats.
- Review every AI-generated claim, especially health and before/after.
- Label synthetic people and voices where required.
- If someone uses AI to fake your content or face, report it to the platform with evidence.

## Lesson 17.5: How Helix applies this
Helix uses rules for maths, cheap models for reading, stronger models for judgement, and always asks before changing anything (see the product design doc).
