# Module 30: Seasonal sales, step by step

**Outcome:** for any big date in the year, you know what to do 10 weeks out, 6 weeks out, 3 weeks out, 2 weeks out, while the sale runs and the week after, one small step at a time.

**Related:** [Module 13 Promotions and sales](13-promotions-and-sales.md), [Module 10 Email, SMS and messaging](10-email-sms-whatsapp.md), [Module 14 Stock and cash](14-inventory-cash-planning.md)


<!-- plain:start -->
> **In plain words**
>
> **Stage:** Mixed. Lesson 30.2 is Attract, 30.1, 30.3, 30.4 and 30.5 are Convert, and 30.6 is Grow. See [Attract, Convert, Grow](stages.md).
>
> **What it is:** A simple timeline for sale days like Black Friday, Christmas, Mother's Day or Diwali. Helix shows each step on Today at the right time.
>
> **Why it matters:** Big sale days reward the stores that got ready early. A little work each week beats a mad rush in the last few days.
>
> **Do this:**
>
> 1. About 10 weeks out, pick your offer and order stock.
> 2. About 6 weeks out, ask a few creators to try your product.
> 3. About 3 weeks out, make your discount code. About 2 weeks out, dress your store and write your emails.
>
> **Words to know:**
>
> - **Offer:** What the buyer gets, such as 20% off, a free gift or a bundle.
> - **Discount code:** A word buyers type at checkout to get the offer.
> - **Creator:** A person with followers who can show your product to them.
> - **Warm audience:** People who already know you, such as past visitors, followers and your email list.
>
> All words are explained in the [glossary](../glossary.md).

## Lesson 30.1: Plan your offer and order stock
<!-- stage:convert -->

**Why this matters:** Stock takes weeks to arrive, and an offer you rush is often one that loses money. Deciding early keeps you calm and keeps your profit.

**Step 1.** Open Numbers in Helix. Write down your profit on one order before ads. Helix shows it in Profit setup.

**Expected result:** You have one number, such as "$18 profit per order before ads".

**Step 2.** Pick one simple offer. Keep the discount smaller than half of that profit, so each sale still pays for its ad. A free gift or a bundle often costs you less than a big discount.

**Expected result:** One offer written down, such as "15% off everything" or "free tote over $60".

**Step 3.** Look at how many of your best seller you sell on a normal day. Plan for about three times that on each sale day, and order now so it lands at least two weeks early.

**Expected result:** Your stock order is placed, with an arrival date before the sale.

**Watch out:** Do not order on hope. Base the order on your own sales, then add a safe buffer.

## Lesson 30.2: Line up creators
<!-- stage:attract -->

**Why this matters:** People trust people. A creator showing your product to their followers before the sale warms up buyers who have never heard of you.

**Step 1.** Make a list of 10 small creators in your niche, with 2,000 to 50,000 followers. Look for people whose followers comment and ask questions.

**Expected result:** A list of 10 names with a link to each profile.

**Step 2.** Send each one a short, friendly message. Use the "Message to a creator" template in Helix's template library and change the [brackets].

**Expected result:** 10 messages sent.

**Step 3.** Gift your product to the ones who say yes. Give each a personal code, such as their name plus 15, so you can see who brought sales. Offer a share of each sale they bring.

**Expected result:** Each creator has the product, a code and a date to post before the sale.

**Watch out:** Never pay for a post you have not seen a sample of. Start with gifts and a share of sales.

## Lesson 30.3: Set up a discount code in Shopify
<!-- stage:convert -->

**Why this matters:** A code that is ready, tested and set to start and stop by itself means no late-night scramble on the big day.

**Step 1.** In Shopify admin, open **Discounts** in the left menu. Press **Create discount**.

**Expected result:** A box asks you to pick a discount type.

**Step 2.** Pick **Amount off products** for a discount on some products, or **Amount off order** for the whole cart.

**Expected result:** The discount form opens.

**Step 3.** Under **Method**, keep **Discount code**. Type your code, such as BFCM26. Short codes are easier to type.

**Expected result:** Your code shows at the top of the form.

**Step 4.** Under **Value**, pick **Percentage** and type your number, such as 15.

**Expected result:** The summary on the right says "15% off".

**Step 5.** Under **Active dates**, set the start date and time, then tick **Set end date** and add the end. Use your store's time zone.

**Expected result:** The summary shows the start and end dates.

**Step 6.** Press **Save**. Then place a test order with the code (you can cancel and refund it) to check it works.

**Expected result:** The code works at checkout and the discount is right.

**Watch out:** If Helix prepared the code for you, it waits in Waiting for your OK. Helix never turns on a discount or changes your store without your approval.

## Lesson 30.4: Dress your store, warm up and write your emails
<!-- stage:convert -->

**Why this matters:** Shoppers decide fast. When the store looks ready for the day and your emails are written, every visit has a clear path to buy.

**Step 1.** Add a homepage banner with the offer and the end date.

**Expected result:** The first thing a visitor sees is the offer.

**Step 2.** Make one collection for the event, such as "Gifts under $50", and add an announcement bar at the top of every page.

**Expected result:** One click from the banner takes people to the collection.

**Step 3.** Write a short gift guide: three to six products, one line each on who it suits.

**Expected result:** The guide is live and linked from the banner.

**Step 4.** Warm up your audience. Post about the coming sale, and ask people to join your email list for early access.

**Expected result:** New sign-ups each day before the sale.

**Step 5.** Write three emails: "It starts tomorrow", "It's on" and "Last day". Use the sale email templates in Helix.

**Expected result:** Three emails are scheduled in your email tool.

**Watch out:** Do not change ads, prices or the store on the sale day itself. Make every change before, and test it.

## Lesson 30.5: Run the event: check your numbers each day
<!-- stage:convert -->

**Why this matters:** During a sale small problems cost real money fast. A five-minute check each morning catches them early.

**Step 1.** Open Numbers in Helix each morning and tap yesterday.

**Expected result:** You see sales, ad spend and profit for the day.

**Step 2.** Compare your ROAS with your break-even ROAS on the same screen. If ROAS is above it, the ads are paying their way.

**Expected result:** You know if each ad dollar made money yesterday.

**Step 3.** Check stock on your best sellers and reply to any customer messages.

**Expected result:** Nothing has sold out by surprise and no message waits more than a day.

**Watch out:** If an ad is below break-even two days in a row, lower its budget a little in your ad account. Do not make big changes in a rush.

## Lesson 30.6: Review the results
<!-- stage:grow -->

**Why this matters:** The best plan for next year is written the week after this one, while you still remember what happened.

**Step 1.** In Numbers, add up the event days: sales, orders, ad spend and profit.

**Expected result:** One line with the totals.

**Step 2.** Write three things that worked and three you would change.

**Expected result:** A short note you can read next year.

**Step 3.** Send a thank-you email to everyone who bought. Use the "Thank-you after a sale" template.

**Expected result:** Buyers feel looked after, and more of them come back.

**Watch out:** Count profit, not just sales. A big sales day that loses money is not a win.
