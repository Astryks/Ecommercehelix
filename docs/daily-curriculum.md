# Daily curriculum: one lesson, one topic, one action

Helix delivers the whole programme as a daily habit. Each day has **one short lesson**, **one topic** and **one clear action**, plus the offer: *I'll do it for you if you want.* Tapping that creates an approval; nothing changes until you approve.

The sequence is ordered by store stage: numbers first, then conversion, retention, creative and ads, more channels, promotions and peak, then scale. Stores that already have a stage covered can mark days done and move on. After the last day the daily loop continues with tasks raised by [Insights](audit-engine.md) and the weekly rhythm from the [Compound Plan](compound-daily-plan.md).

This file is generated from `scripts/curriculum_source.py` by `python3 scripts/build_curriculum.py`, which also writes `src/lib/seed/curriculum.json` for the app (Today screen and roadmap timeline).

**Do it for me** tiers: Free, Starter or Growth show the minimum plan for Helix to do the work. Days marked *Guide* are done by you with the checklist.

## Day detail

### Day 1: Set up your cost drivers

*Topic: Profit, not revenue · Stage 1 · 10 minutes · SOP 02*

**Lesson.** Revenue tells you how busy you were. Contribution profit tells you if it was worth it: sales minus product cost, shipping, fees and marketing. Today you give Helix the costs it needs to calculate that every day.

**Action.**

1. Open the Scorecard settings card.
2. Enter monthly fixed costs (staff, software, rent, your wage).
3. Enter a monthly revenue target you believe in.
4. Enter a target MER, or use 30% for now.
5. Save.

**I'll do it for you:** Pre-fill cost drivers from your store data (Free plan, with your approval).

### Day 2: Enter yesterday's numbers

*Topic: The daily scorecard · Stage 1 · 5 minutes · SOP 02*

**Lesson.** A two-minute scorecard each morning is the heartbeat of a growing store. You will see contribution profit, MER and your flags before you make any decision.

**Action.**

1. Open the Scorecard and add yesterday: orders, sales, discounts, refunds.
2. Add product cost, shipping and payment fees.
3. Add ad spend for each channel.
4. Check the green, amber and red flags.
5. Write one line on what you will do today.

**I'll do it for you:** Sync yesterday's numbers automatically (Starter plan, with your approval).

### Day 3: Work out your break-even ROAS and CPA

*Topic: Break-even targets · Stage 1 · 10 minutes · SOP 02*

**Lesson.** Every ad decision needs a line in the sand. Break-even ROAS is 1 ÷ (1 − variable cost %). Break-even CPA is what you can spend to win an order without losing money on it.

**Action.**

1. Open the Scorecard and read your variable cost ratio.
2. Note break-even ROAS and break-even CPA from the channel card.
3. Set a good CPA for cold traffic (about AOV × target MER × 2).
4. Write all three on a sticky note near your desk.

**I'll do it for you:** not for this one. It is quick to do yourself and you learn the most by doing it.

### Day 4: Diagnose what is holding profit back

*Topic: Find the constraint · Stage 1 · 15 minutes · SOP 03*

**Lesson.** Growth stalls for one of three reasons: costs are too high, conversion is too low, or there is not enough traffic. Fixing the wrong one wastes months.

**Action.**

1. Compare your numbers with the healthy ranges in Lesson 1.4.
2. Mark which KPI is furthest out of range.
3. Pick the matching situation in Lesson 2.2.
4. Choose one fix to work on this week.

**I'll do it for you:** Run the diagnosis on your data (Starter plan, with your approval).
