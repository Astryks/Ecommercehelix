### Facebook group

Day 1 of 72 · Attract · The brand core

Why it matters
Your brand core is a one-page summary of who you are. Everything you write and post comes from it. Brands without one often end up competing on price and discounts. Brands with one need fewer discounts, because trust does the selling.

Step by step
1. Describe 2 or 3 types of customer. Note their goals, what bugs them and where they spend time online.
   What good looks like: You have 2 or 3 short customer descriptions.
2. Write your brand promise in one sentence (why choose us).
   What good looks like: You have one promise sentence.
3. Pick three words for your personality and three words you never use.
   What good looks like: You have a six-word voice guide.
4. Write two or three values, each with one thing you actually do that proves it.
   What good looks like: Every value has proof, not just a claim.
5. Write your story in under 100 words: why the brand exists. Then save all five answers on one page.
   What good looks like: A one-page brand core is saved where your team, freelancers and AI tools can read it.

Key takeaway
Brands with one need fewer discounts, because trust does the selling.
