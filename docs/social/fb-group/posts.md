## 2. Black Friday prep checklist 🛍️
*Image: post-bf-checklist.png*

Black Friday comes fast. Do these things early, so the big day feels calm:
- ☐ **Pick your offer.** Work out your profit after the discount. If you would lose money, pick a smaller offer.
- ☐ **Count your stock.** Make sure you have enough of your best sellers.
- ☐ **Test your checkout.** Buy something on your phone, start to finish.
- ☐ **Write your emails now.** One to say it is coming, one when it starts, one "last chance".
- ☐ **Plan your ads.** Choose your budget and an end date.
- ☐ **Check your shipping times.** Tell shoppers when orders will arrive.

Which one will you tick off this week? Tell us below 👇

## 4. A simple ad budget rule for beginners 💵
*Image: post-ad-budget.png*

New to ads? Use this rule: **Start small. Learn first.**
1. Pick a daily amount you are happy to lose while you learn, like $10 to $20 a day.
2. Know the most you can pay to get one sale and still make money. Example: you sell a $40 product and keep $15 after costs. If one sale costs you more than $15 in ads, you lose money.
3. Wait a few days before you change things. Ads need time to learn.

When ads make you money, raise the budget slowly. What daily budget are you starting with?

## 5. How to write a product description that sells ✍️
Copy this simple template:

1. **Who it is for:** "Made for ___ who want ___."
2. **The big benefit:** What does it make better or easier?
3. **3 quick points:** Size, what it is made of, what is in the box.
4. **Clear the worry:** Shipping time, returns, how to care for it.
5. **Ask them to act:** "Add to cart today."

Example: "Made for dog owners who hate messy walks. This bag holder clips on any lead and fits a full roll of bags. Ships in 2 to 4 days. Easy 30-day returns."
*(Only promise what is true for your store.)*

Try it on one product, then share it below for friendly feedback!
