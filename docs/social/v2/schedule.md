## Order 5 — Post 5: Plan by margin, not just % off.

- **When:** 2026-10-18 19:00 Australia/Sydney
- **Media:** `/workspace/reports/helix/social-v2/animated/post05-margin-offers.mp4` (mp4)

### Instagram

```
"Let's just do 20% off" might be the most expensive sentence of BFCM.
Take a $50 item with $18 product cost, $8 shipping and $12 ad cost per order ($38 in total), plus ~3% payment fees:
• 10% off → $45 price − $38 − $1.35 fees = $5.65 profit per order
• 20% off → $40 price − $38 − $1.20 fees = $0.80 profit per order
Doubling the discount cut profit per order by about 86%. Run your own numbers before you pick the discount. Bundles, gifts with purchase or free shipping thresholds can sometimes protect margin better.
Example numbers only. ecommercehelix.com

## Order 8 — Post 8: Your break-even ROAS

- **When:** 2026-10-21 19:00 Australia/Sydney
- **Media:** `/workspace/reports/helix/social-v2/static/post08-breakeven-roas.png` (png)

### Instagram

```
Before you judge an ad, work out your break-even ROAS (return on ad spend).

Take what's left after product, shipping and fees: $60 − $20 − $7 − $1.80 = $31.20.

Break-even ROAS = price ÷ that amount = $60 ÷ $31.20 ≈ 1.92.

Below 1.92 you lose money on each ad-driven order. Above it you're in profit. Example numbers, so use your own.

Link in bio: ecommercehelix.com
